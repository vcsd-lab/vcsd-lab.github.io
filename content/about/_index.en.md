The VCSDL Laboratory is led by **Prof. Chih-Hung Kuo** and was established in **September 2005**.  
The laboratory currently consists of **28 master’s students**.

We are located in **Room 95515, 5th Floor, Chi Mei Building, National Cheng Kung University**.

We warmly welcome students with strong interest in the following areas:

- Quantization and compression of large language models
- Digital integrated circuit design
- Genomic sequence alignment algorithms
- Image recognition algorithms

Students who are interested in visiting the laboratory are encouraged to contact us by email in advance to schedule an appropriate time.
